EMBER DUEL

A battle of suits. Outplay Mara, card by card.

Both duelists start at 30 life. Each turn you draw a card and your ember refills.

Play cards to strike, raise guard, or mend wounds. Guard soaks damage first.

Harmony: a card matching the suit of the card you just played costs 1 less ember.

Reduce Mara to 0 life to win. Fall to 0 and the duel is lost.

Click a card to play it · keys 1–7 play a card · Enter ends your turn · N starts a new duel
